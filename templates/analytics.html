Climate Analytics Engine

How SkyCast turns 1.4 TB of raw climate data into a live forecast.

Powered by Big Data

SkyCast doesn't just fetch simple APIs. Our architecture utilizes a simulated Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) to ingest and process massive volumes of historical climate data.

By leveraging MapReduce principles, we aggregate decades of atmospheric pressure and temperature patterns. This data trains our predictive models before syncing securely with our Flask API endpoints.
